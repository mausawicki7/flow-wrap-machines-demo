Horizontal flow-wrap (HFFS) is where D.T. built its reputation. These machines wrap
individual products in a sealed film pillow pack — the format you see on virtually
every packaged snack on a retail shelf.

Commonly packed on this range:

- Bars and cookies
- Candies and lollipops
- Nuts and chocolate
- Chewing gum
- Breadsticks and dry baked goods
- Soap bars, pencils and promotional items

Every machine is imported from a selected supplier and upgraded in our own plant with
North American and German components before it ships. CE certified.
